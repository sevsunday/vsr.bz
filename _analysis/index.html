    Switch-back timing

    Each panel is one player's switch-backs on the chosen slow weapon: a slow shot, a faster weapon fired in between, then the slow shot again. Each switch-back lands in one cell. Across is the margin: how long after its reload the slow weapon fired. The left edge is the instant it was ready. Up is fire lost: how long the fast weapon sat ready but unfired before that slow shot. The bottom edge means it kept firing.

    Brighter cells hold more switch-backs. The dashed lines mark 50 ms, and the last row and column hold everything beyond. The four corner figures add up to 100%, so a strong panel is one bright spot in the bottom-left corner.

    Best first orders the panels by the bottom-left figure, the same ranking as the table below. Players with fewer than 30 switch-backs follow, not ranked. Held cycles, under each panel, are the slow weapon refiring with nothing in between; they are counted but not drawn.
